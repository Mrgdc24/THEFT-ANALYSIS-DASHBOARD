SUMMARY OF MY PROJECT

1	🔴  Highest-Theft Region	Auckland leads with 1,638 thefts — 36.0% of all NZ vehicle thefts over the 6-month period.
		
2	📍  Highest Theft Rate	Gisborne has the highest theft rate at 337.8 thefts per 100,000 residents, despite having a smaller total count — indicating disproportionate risk relative to population.
		
3	🚗  Most Targeted Vehicle Type	Station wagons are the single most-stolen vehicle type (945 thefts, 20.9% of total), closely followed by saloons and hatchbacks — together comprising over 50% of all thefts.
		
4	📈  Rising Theft Trend	Theft incidents rose from 464 in October 2021 to 1,053 in March 2022 — a 126.9% increase — with March 2022 being the peak month (1,053 thefts). Escalating trend demands urgent police patrol intervention.
		
5	💎  Luxury vs Standard	Standard vehicles account for 95.8% of thefts (4,348) vs luxury at 4.2% (190). Luxury vehicles are targeted at a much lower absolute rate, though their higher replacement value amplifies insurance exposure.
		
6	🏭  Most Stolen Make	Toyota is the most-stolen brand with 716 incidents, reflecting its large market share in NZ. Toyota, Nissan, and Ford feature prominently in the top 10, all of which are high-prevalence Standard vehicles.
		
7	🗺️  Population Density Effect	High-density regions (Auckland: 343 pop/km²) show the highest absolute theft volumes. However, lower-density regions like Gisborne (6.2 pop/km²) show elevated per-capita theft rates, suggesting patrol resources should not focus solely on urban centres.
		
8	📋  Insurance Recommendations	Insurers should apply risk-adjusted premiums for: (1) Station wagons & saloons in Auckland and Gisborne; (2) older model-year vehicles (pre-2005) which feature heavily in theft data; (3) silver/white vehicles, the most commonly stolen colours.
		
9	👮  Police Patrol Planning	Recommend increased patrols in Auckland, Canterbury, Bay of Plenty, and Wellington — which together account for over 70% of all reported thefts. Focus hours on evening/night patterns given the theft distribution data.
		
10	📅  Data Period Note	Analysis covers 07 October 2021 to 06 April 2022 — a total of 4,553 recorded vehicle theft incidents across 13 of 16 New Zealand regions.
